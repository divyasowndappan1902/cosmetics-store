Our mission

To make everyday beauty gentle, honest and inclusive, so that every person can find a shade and a routine that feels like theirs

We design for Indian skin first: humidity, sun, pollution and a wide range of undertones. Every product is tested on real skin across these conditions before it reaches you.

What we stand for

Three promises we check every product against.

Honest labels

We list every ingredient and its job, and we never hide behind the word "fragrance".

Skin first

Our makeup includes actives like niacinamide and squalane, so colour also cares for your skin.

Kind to the planet

Recyclable glass, refills and plastic-free shipping wherever we can.

Our promise
